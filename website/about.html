About the Experiment & AI Governance Framework

Empirical research into autonomous execution of human-designed strategies under real market dynamics.

Core Research Framework

Primary Question: "Can a human-designed investment strategy, autonomously executed by an AI-managed virtual investment company using real-time market information, generate sustainable risk-adjusted returns in the Nepalese equity market?"

Secondary Question: "Does AI-based autonomous execution improve, maintain, or deteriorate the performance of the underlying human-designed investment strategy?"

"I designed the strategy. The AI operated the company. The market judged the strategy."
